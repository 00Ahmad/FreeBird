# Code of Conduct

FreeBird is a friendly project. We want everyone who uses it or contributes to it, whatever their background or experience, to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers; everyone was new once.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (for example via the repository's **Security** tab for sensitive reports), or open an issue if it's not sensitive.

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
